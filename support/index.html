How to create and open your plumbing website

This walkthrough follows the same screens you see when you build with PlumbersWebsites.com — from the homepage through the website builder to your dashboard. No technical background required.

BUILD MY WEBSITE → Jump to the steps ↓

Eight steps from start to your website

After you sign in, the builder walks you through four sections — your business, location and services, availability, and design — with a live preview on the right. Use Save & Exit in the builder header if you need to pause and come back later.

  1. 1 Start building
  2. 2 Enter your email
  3. 3 Check your email
  4. 4 Your Business
  5. 5 Where & What
  6. 6 Availability
  7. 7 Choose Your Design
  8. 8 Your website is ready

1 Start building

Visit PlumbersWebsites.com and begin

On the homepage, choose Build My Website (or Build It in the header). You will be asked for your email first so we can send a secure sign-in link — no password to create or remember.

2 Enter your email

Request your one-time sign-in link

Enter the email address you want to use for your account, then select Email me my sign-in link. We send a secure link you can use instead of a password.

  • Use an inbox you can open on the same device where you started.
  • No password is required to sign in.

3 Check your email

Open the sign-in link on this device

After you submit your email, you will see a Check your email message. If your address is eligible, we send a secure sign-in link — open that email on the same device and use the sign-in button in the message to enter the website builder.

Wrong address? Select Use a different email to go back and try again.

4 Your Business

Name, phone, and trust details

Step 1 in the builder is Your Business. Fill in the essentials your customers need to reach you, and add optional trust labels that appear on your preview.

  • Business name and phone number are required.
  • Email address is optional but helps if you want it on your site.
  • Under Trust & Experience, tap the tags that apply — for example Licensed, Insured, Est. 2022, Locally owned, or Family owned.
  • Select Continue to Step 2 when you are ready. The preview updates as you type.

5 Where & What

Location and services

Step 2 is Where & What. Tell us where you work and which plumbing services you offer. Your preview uses this for local wording and your services list.

  • Enter where your business is based (for example, Tampa, FL).
  • Use + Add area if you serve additional towns or regions.
  • Under Services you offer, check every service you want on your site. Options are grouped (repairs, drains, water heaters, and more).
  • The header shows how many services you selected — pick the ones that match what you actually provide.

6 Availability

Hours and emergency service

Step 3 is Availability. Choose the regular hours and emergency option that best match how you operate. Your preview shows these details near your call button.

  • Regular hours — pick a preset such as Mon–Fri 8 AM–5 PM, Mon–Sat 8 AM–6 PM, Every day 9 AM–9 PM, or Custom hours.
  • Emergency service — choose No emergency service, Same day urgent calls, Evenings & weekends, or 24/7 emergency service.
  • Continue to Choose Your Design when finished.

7 Choose Your Design

Compare designs and generate your site

Step 4 is Choose Your Design. You can compare six styles — Precision Pro, Modern Volt, Bright Current, Skyline, Evergreen, and Radius — and see each one in the live preview. The builder notes that you can change the design later.

  • Select the design you prefer with the radio button next to its thumbnail.
  • Use Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile toggles above the preview to check how it looks.
  • When you are happy, select Generate My Website. The builder explains that it will create your complete five-page website using the information you entered.

8 Your website is ready

Open your site from the dashboard

After you select Generate My Website in the design step, you arrive at My Website Dashboard. The dashboard shows your business name and a message that your website is ready to explore.

  • Select View My Website to open your website.
  • Your website address is displayed beneath the button (for example, tampaplumbing.plumberswebsites.com on the sample dashboard).
  • Return to the dashboard anytime by signing in again with your email link.

TROUBLESHOOTING

Did not receive your sign-in email?

  • Wait a few minutes — delivery can be delayed.
  • Check your spam, junk, or promotions folder.
  • The sign-in link expires in about 15 minutes and works once. Request a fresh link if it expired or was already used.
  • Open the link on the same device where you started, as shown on the Check your email screen.
  • Use Use a different email if you mistyped your address.
