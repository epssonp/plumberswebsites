WHY WE EXIST

Your Website Shouldn't Become Another Job

Creating a website from scratch can turn into extra work on top of running a plumbing business: deciding which pages you need, organizing services, writing content and making design choices — often while learning unfamiliar website tools.

PlumbersWebsites.com offers a different starting point: a plumbing-focused structure and design choices, with personalization driven by the accurate business information you provide — not a blank canvas you have to figure out alone.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

We Build the Foundation. You Make It Yours.

This is the principle behind the product. We prepare the plumbing website framework; you supply factual details about your business and choose how the site should look. We do not invent testimonials, reviews, ratings, awards, credentials, years of experience, guarantees or service areas — and we do not independently verify licences, insurance or other claims you choose to display.

YOUR DETAILS

Your Business Information Comes First

Your website should represent your actual business, not an invented version of it. Supported information you can supply includes business name, phone, email when you choose to provide it, services, service areas, business hours and emergency availability. These details personalize relevant sections of the site. Not every field appears on every page, and we do not independently verify the accuracy of what you submit.
