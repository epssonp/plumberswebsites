Frequently Asked Questions

Thinking about getting a website for your plumbing business? Here are the answers to the questions we hear most often.

What is a plumber website builder?

PlumbersWebsites is a done-for-you website service designed specifically for plumbing businesses. Instead of building a website yourself, you provide your business details, choose a design and preview your personalised website before deciding whether to pay.

What do I get with my plumbing website?

You get a personalised five-page website covering Home, Services, Our Approach, About and Contact. Your website is built around the business information you provide, including your services, contact details and service areas.

Do I have to pay before I see my website?

No. You can enter your business information, choose one of our six designs and preview your personalised website before choosing a paid plan. You can see what you're getting before committing.

Do I need technical skills or coding experience?

Not at all. You don't need to write code or learn how to use a complicated website builder. Simply provide your business details and follow the steps to create your website preview.

Can I choose how my website looks?

Yes. You can explore six professionally designed plumbing website layouts and choose the one you prefer. Your business information is then used to personalise the website around your plumbing business.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. The website designs are built to adapt to different screen sizes, including desktop computers, tablets and mobile phones, so customers can find your services and contact information on the device they use.

How much does a plumbing website cost?

You can choose between two plans: $49 per month, billed monthly, or $399 per year, billed annually. The annual plan saves $189 compared with paying $49 each month for 12 months.

You can preview your personalised website before selecting a plan and proceeding to payment.

Where will my website be published?

Your website can be published on its own address under the PlumbersWebsites domain. The publishing process will guide you through setting up your website address.

Can I use my own business information?

Absolutely. Your website is personalised using the business details you provide, including your business name, phone number, location, services and other information you choose to supply. We use your details rather than inventing business claims or customer testimonials.

What happens after I preview my website?

Review your website and decide whether you're happy with the design and content. If you want to continue, choose your preferred pricing plan and follow the payment and publishing steps.

You don't need to make a payment simply to see your preview.
