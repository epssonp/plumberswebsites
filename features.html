YOUR WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Your Five Website Pages

Each site includes five in-app views with distinct purposes. Navigation moves between views on your published site; separate shareable URLs for each view are not part of what we describe here.

01

Home

Introduces your plumbing business, highlights selected services and makes it easy for customers to get in touch.

02

Services

Presents the services you offer and helps visitors connect common plumbing problems with relevant services. Any problem-matching helper is a starting point for conversation — not a diagnosis.

03

Our Approach

Explains the expected customer journey, communication and work process using the site's explanatory framework.

04

About

Presents your business identity, service areas and services. Optional profile sections may appear only when you supply the underlying information.

05

Contact

Brings together phone, your supplied email where available, business hours, service areas and the message-form interface where provided on your site.
