## Executive summary

PlumbersWebsites.com’s **six Academy category titles exist only in planning** (Phase 1F brief and this review). They are **not implemented** in `academy.html` (empty stub), navigation, or HTML metadata. Phase 1C–1E keyword work is **strongest for Category 5 (websites & digital tools)** and **moderate for Category 4 (local growth / GBP / owner SEO)**. Categories **1, 2, 3, and 6** are strategically important for a ~300-article library but **lack validated US volume evidence** in the current research corpus.

| 6 | Industry Intelligence & Professional Development | **Industry Trends & Professional Growth** | Revise title + new description |

Commercial keyword ownership from Phase 1E is **unchanged**. Category 5 must **support**, not **compete with**, the homepage primary `plumber website builder` and `/features/` primary `plumbing website design`.

## Category 5 worked example (approved direction)

**Previous title:** Websites, Digital Tools & Online Presence  

**Agreed working title:** **Plumbing Websites & Digital Tools**

**Agreed working description:**  
*Practical guides for plumbing business owners on building a plumbing website, choosing website templates and digital tools, improving their online presence, and helping potential customers find their business online.*

**Why this direction works (applied to other categories):**

- Industry and subject are explicit (“Plumbing Websites”).
- Breadth covers build, templates, visibility, and tools without sounding like a sales page.
- Description states **owner benefit** in natural language.
- Phase 1D supports **description-level** phrases (`plumbing website`, `how to build a plumbing website`, templates, examples) — **not** a claim that the category title itself outranks the homepage head term.

**Cannibalisation guard:** Do **not** use `plumber website builder` as the Category 5 hub H1. Homepage retains primary ownership (Phase 1E).

## Individual category reviews

### Category 1 — Plumbing Techniques, Tools & Equipment

**A. Subject**  
Field craft, tools, equipment, and jobsite technique content for owners and lead techs — quality, safety, and efficiency. **Not** homeowner DIY repair (excluded in Phase 1).

**B. Current title:** Techniques, Technology & Equipment  
- Clear to tradespeople but **“Technology”** collides with Category 5 “Digital Tools.”  
- Generic without “Plumbing” prefix.

**C. Current description:** Not found in repository.

**D. Keywords**  
Phase 1 registry **excludes** consumer repair (`how to fix a leaky faucet`, etc.). **No List A cluster** with Phase 1D volume for owner technique/equipment hubs. **Gap:** dedicated discovery needed before SEO-led hub naming.

**E. Recommendation**

| | |
|--|--|
| **Recommended title** | Plumbing Techniques, Tools & Equipment |
| **Recommended description** | Hands-on guidance for plumbing business owners and field teams on proven techniques, jobsite tools, and equipment choices that affect quality, safety, and efficiency on the job. |
| **Alternative** | Keep current title if editorial prefers original wording — preferred revision reduces Cat 5 ambiguity. |
| **Evidence** | Research gap; exclusions documented |
| **Overlap** | Low vs commercial site; medium vs Cat 5 if “technology” left unqualified |

## Six-category coherence review

**Distinct purposes**

| Cat | Role |
|-----|------|
| 1 | Field work: technique & equipment |
| 2 | Compliance: license, training, credentials |
| 3 | Business operations & startup (non-marketing, non-website-deep) |
| 4 | Customers & local visibility (GBP, SEO, leads) |
| 5 | Websites & digital tools (strongest keyword evidence) |
| 6 | Industry trends & leadership growth |

**Navigation read:** Six titles are parallel in length after revisions; Cat 5 “Plumbing Websites” anchors the product-adjacent pillar explicitly.

**Keyword placement discipline**

- **>100 band terms** from Phase 1D are **not** duplicated across multiple category **titles**.
- `plumber website builder` → commercial homepage, **not** category H1.
- `plumbing website design` → `/features/`, optional in Cat 5 **description** only.
- Cat 4 owns GBP/owner SEO language; Cat 5 owns “plumbing website” build/design/template language.

**Subcategory-level reservations (next phase only)**

- Cat 5: Choose a builder; Templates & examples; Build guides; Cost & ROI; Conversion & hosting.  
- Cat 4: Google Business Profile; Local SEO; Reviews & reputation; Lead generation.  
- Cat 3: Startup checklist; Operations & software.  
- Cat 2: State licensing (by state — research heavy).  
- Cat 1: Tools/equipment guides (research gap).  
- Cat 6: Trends / leadership (research gap).
